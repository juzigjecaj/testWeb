FAQ

Frequent questions answered

Frequent questions answered

Are you an agency or a software product?

We’re a go-to-market agency, not a software product. Software alone leaves your team responsible for execution and results. With Swaioh, experienced people build and run your GTM operations, using software and AI where they add value. We take responsibility for performance by setting clear goals, tracking results, and adjusting the approach when something isn’t working.

Can you guarantee a number of meetings?

We don’t promise a fixed number of meetings or guaranteed pipeline. Hitting a meeting target often means prioritizing volume over account quality. Instead, we align on goals before starting, report on performance, and refine our approach based on what’s working.

What kind of businesses do you work best with?

We work exclusively with B2B companies across client acquisition, investor outreach, and revenue operations. The main requirement is established product-market fit. We’re not here to figure out your offer or test whether your business works. We’re here to scale what’s already working. If you have a proven product, a clear ICP, and a track record of closing deals, we can build a system to help you close more. Deal sizes of $10K or more tend to work best because the economics make sense at that level.

Will this work for investor outreach as well?

The system works the same way across industries. We detect relevant signals to identify potential buyers or investors. For example, when an investor backs a company, they may file a Form D with the SEC. That information is public, but finding and organizing it takes work. Our system scrapes these filings and surfaces investors whose investment thesis aligns with your company. The same approach applies to conference attendees, job postings, company announcements, and other signals. The signals change depending on your industry, but the underlying system stays the same.

Do I own the campaign assets if we work together and I leave?

You own everything that can be cleanly transferred. We provide full access and transparency into the lists, scripts, and campaign data. Upon request, we can also build your email infrastructure so you own it from the start.

How long does it take to launch and see results?

The initial system is typically ready to launch within two weeks, depending on access and approvals. Early positive feedback may come in over the following two weeks. The first 90 days focus on testing the market, refining the offer, and identifying the strongest campaign angles. Meaningful market insights usually emerge around day 60, though commercial results depend on the market and sales cycle.

What exactly does Swaioh manage?

Swaioh manages your outbound operations from strategy through qualified handoff. We handle market definition, account research, data verification, sending infrastructure, positioning, copywriting, campaign launches, daily management, testing, reply qualification, follow-ups, and reporting. Your team approves the commercial direction and takes qualified opportunities forward.

How does Swaioh choose target accounts and buyers?

We start by analyzing your offer economics, strongest proof points, buyer conditions, exclusions, addressable market, and triggers that might prompt an account to act. Then we map relevant companies and stakeholders, validate the initial segments with your team, and use campaign and sales feedback to continuously refine targeting and prioritization.

How is Swaioh different from a lead generation or appointment-setting agency?

We don’t sell contact lists or chase arbitrary meeting targets. Swaioh brings together market strategy, research, infrastructure, campaigns, reply handling, and sales feedback into one coordinated system. Everything is built around a single commercial objective: generating qualified enterprise conversations your team can turn into real opportunities.

What does the client need to provide?

The client provides its strongest case studies, previous market learnings, sales calendar access, commercial context, and timely approvals. If the offer hasn’t been proven with cold traffic, Swaioh helps shape one around the available proof and buyer problem. The client remains responsible for discovery calls, proposals, investor or buyer conversations, negotiations, and closing.
